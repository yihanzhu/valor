What Valor actually says.

Every workflow below, with the output it produced — not a mock-up of it. Pick a phrase the way you would type it at your agent.

These are recordings. A live agent needs a model at runtime, and this site has no backend — so the transcripts were captured by running Valor's own command specs against the seeded demo profile in the repo: a fictional engineer, fictional tickets, invented colleagues. The review console further down is not a recording — it's the real artifact, fully interactive. Want the whole thing live against your own work? That's two commands, at the bottom of this page.
