# Career Framework — Growth Stages (no formal levels)

> **Example framework.** For teams or individuals without a formal leveled
> ladder. Instead of level codes, it describes three growth stages you can
> self-assess against. Copy it to `~/.valor/career_framework.md`, then rename
> the stages, edit the descriptions, and replace the values to fit your
> situation. Valor still needs a `## Levels` section, so the stages live there.

## Levels

### Developing - Building Core Competence

**Role summary:** Establishing a foundation. Focused on doing the core work
well and learning the team's tools, patterns, and expectations.

**Competencies:**

- **Subject Matter Expertise:** Builds solid fundamentals in the craft; produces correct, reviewable work in the primary toolset.
- **Industry Knowledge:** Learns the standard tools and methods of the field; follows established practice.
- **Internal Collaboration:** Communicates clearly, asks good questions, and acts on feedback.
- **Autonomy & Scope:** Delivers well-defined tasks with guidance; escalates blockers promptly.
- **Leadership:** Owns their commitments and does what they say they will do.

### Established - Independent Contributor

**Role summary:** A dependable, self-directed contributor who owns meaningful
work end to end and needs little oversight.

**Competencies:**

- **Subject Matter Expertise:** Handles complex work in their area; makes sound trade-offs and improves what they touch.
- **Industry Knowledge:** Chooses appropriate tools and approaches; evaluates new ones against real needs.
- **Internal Collaboration:** Gives useful feedback to peers; coordinates across roles on scope and sequencing.
- **Autonomy & Scope:** Turns ambiguous problems into a plan and executes independently; owns an area of work.
- **Leadership:** Helps others get unblocked; raises the quality bar by example.

### Leading - Multiplier

**Role summary:** Extends impact beyond their own output by shaping direction
and making the people and work around them better.

**Competencies:**

- **Subject Matter Expertise:** Deep expertise others rely on; designs across multiple pieces of work and anticipates problems.
- **Industry Knowledge:** Brings in outside practice and sets standards others adopt.
- **Internal Collaboration:** Aligns multiple people and stakeholders around a shared plan; resolves cross-cutting friction.
- **Autonomy & Scope:** Identifies the highest-value work and drives it; owns outcomes across a broad surface.
- **Leadership:** Mentors and multiplies others; influences direction and culture.

## Guidance

- These stages describe growth, not job titles — you can sit between two stages
  or lead in one axis while developing in another.
- Use them to name where you are per competency and what "the next stage" looks
  like, then let coaching reference those descriptions directly.
