## Levels

### L3 - Software Engineer

**Role summary:** Delivers well-scoped features and fixes on an established
codebase. Growing from guided execution toward independent delivery of
day-to-day tasks.

**Competencies:**

- **Subject Matter Expertise:** Writes correct, readable code in the team's primary language and stack; adds tests and follows existing patterns.
- **Industry Knowledge:** Knows the team's core tools and frameworks; learns new ones with support.
- **Internal Collaboration:** Communicates progress and blockers clearly; incorporates code-review feedback constructively.
- **Autonomy & Scope:** Completes clearly-defined tasks with guidance; asks for help before getting stuck for long.
- **Leadership:** Takes ownership of assigned work; keeps commitments and flags risks early.

### L4 - Senior Software Engineer

**Role summary:** Owns delivery of medium features end to end, from design
through rollout. A reliable, largely self-directed contributor on the team.

**Competencies:**

- **Subject Matter Expertise:** Designs and implements components with sound trade-offs; writes maintainable code and improves what they touch.
- **Industry Knowledge:** Applies established patterns and tooling appropriately; evaluates new approaches against real needs.
- **Internal Collaboration:** Gives thoughtful code reviews; coordinates with adjacent engineers and product on scope and sequencing.
- **Autonomy & Scope:** Breaks ambiguous problems into a plan and executes with little oversight; owns a feature area.
- **Leadership:** Mentors newer engineers; raises the quality bar through reviews and examples.

### L5 - Staff Engineer

**Role summary:** Drives technical direction for a team or a cross-cutting
problem. Delivers impact through design, influence, and multiplying others.

**Competencies:**

- **Subject Matter Expertise:** Designs systems spanning multiple components; anticipates failure modes, scaling, and operational concerns.
- **Industry Knowledge:** Brings in relevant industry practices; sets technical standards the team adopts.
- **Internal Collaboration:** Aligns multiple engineers and stakeholders around a shared technical plan; resolves cross-team dependencies.
- **Autonomy & Scope:** Identifies and scopes high-value work independently; owns outcomes across a team's surface area.
- **Leadership:** Multiplies the team through mentoring, design leadership, and pragmatic decisions; unblocks others.

### L6 - Principal Engineer

**Role summary:** Sets technical strategy across teams and drives outcomes that
matter at the organization level. Influence extends well beyond a single team.

**Competencies:**

- **Subject Matter Expertise:** Recognized authority in a major domain; makes architecture decisions with long-term, org-wide consequences.
- **Industry Knowledge:** Tracks the field ahead of need; guides technology bets and long-range investment.
- **Internal Collaboration:** Builds alignment across teams and leadership; negotiates trade-offs among competing priorities.
- **Autonomy & Scope:** Defines the most important problems to solve; owns ambiguous, high-stakes initiatives across teams.
- **Leadership:** Grows other senior engineers; shapes engineering culture and raises the bar broadly.

## Guidance

- **Years of experience (guideline):** L3 = 0-2 years, L4 = 2-5 years, L5 = 5+ years.
- **Time in role before promotion:** typically 1-2 years demonstrating the next level's behaviors consistently.
