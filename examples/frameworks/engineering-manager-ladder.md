# Career Framework — Engineering Management

> **Example framework.** A generic people-management ladder for engineering,
> with placeholder company values. Copy it to `~/.valor/career_framework.md`,
> then edit the level codes, descriptions, and values to match your own
> organization. The five competency axes are shared with the IC ladder so
> coaching stays consistent across tracks.

## Levels

### M3 - Engineering Manager

**Role summary:** Manages a single team, owning its delivery, health, and the
growth of its engineers. Balances hands-on technical judgment with people
leadership.

**Competencies:**

- **Subject Matter Expertise:** Retains enough technical depth to review designs, assess trade-offs, and coach on execution.
- **Industry Knowledge:** Aware of engineering practices and hiring trends relevant to the team's domain.
- **Internal Collaboration:** Partners with product and adjacent teams on roadmap and dependencies; communicates status upward and outward.
- **Autonomy & Scope:** Runs one team's planning and delivery with little oversight; owns team-level outcomes.
- **Leadership:** Sets clear expectations, gives regular feedback, and supports each report's growth; handles performance conversations.

### M4 - Senior Engineering Manager

**Role summary:** Manages a larger team or multiple teams, often including
managing other managers or tech leads. Owns outcomes across a broader area.

**Competencies:**

- **Subject Matter Expertise:** Guides technical strategy across teams without owning every detail; ensures sound architecture and operational rigor.
- **Industry Knowledge:** Applies organizational and process practices to scale teams effectively; benchmarks against the field.
- **Internal Collaboration:** Aligns multiple teams and stakeholders on shared goals; resolves cross-team conflict and dependencies.
- **Autonomy & Scope:** Owns planning and delivery for a group of teams; defines how the area operates.
- **Leadership:** Develops managers and senior engineers; builds a healthy, inclusive culture and a strong hiring bar.

### M5 - Director of Engineering

**Role summary:** Leads a department or function. Sets multi-team strategy,
owns organizational health, and connects engineering execution to business
outcomes.

**Competencies:**

- **Subject Matter Expertise:** Ensures technical strategy across the org is coherent and sustainable; makes high-stakes build/buy and platform decisions.
- **Industry Knowledge:** Anticipates industry shifts and org-design needs; guides long-range investment.
- **Internal Collaboration:** Partners with executive peers across functions; represents engineering in company-level planning.
- **Autonomy & Scope:** Owns outcomes for a department; defines the goals other leaders execute against.
- **Leadership:** Builds and scales the leadership bench; shapes culture, values, and long-term organizational health.

## Guidance

- **Years of experience (guideline):** M3 = first-line manager, M4 = manages managers or a large team, M5 = department leader.
- **Time in role before promotion:** typically 2+ years demonstrating the next level's scope and outcomes.
